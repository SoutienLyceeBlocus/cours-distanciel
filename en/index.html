Why

Fighting for a better future should not cost anyone their studies.

We are a group of volunteers. Our goal: to guarantee access to education for every high school student who demonstrates and joins the blockade.

Studying is a right, but certainly not a privilege. No young person should have to choose between fighting for a better future and continuing their studies.

The project rests on mutual help between students: free academic support, with lessons, tutorials and playful sessions, to revise with peace of mind during the blockade.

The platform

Lessons by subject, year and specialty.

Volunteers offer sessions from 30 minutes to 2 hours, in both the general and the technological tracks. Each student first sees the lessons that match their year and their specialty subjects.

Each lesson has its own discussion group. The volunteer shares the video call link there, and students ask their questions before the lesson.

Subjects and times depend on our volunteer tutors: the schedule fills up as they offer lessons.

Volunteers are invited by the team, then approved one by one before they can offer a lesson.

How it works

Four steps, no real name required.

  1. Create an accountA username, an email address, the track and the specialty subjects. The login link arrives by email.
  2. Pick a lessonFrom the schedule for the next two weeks, filtered by subject, year, location, time.
  3. Join the groupSigning up for a lesson opens its discussion group.
    In remote The video call link is shared.
    In face-to-face : The precise address is shared.
  4. Attend the lessonUnder a username, camera optional. The site rules forbid recording lessons.

Safety and privacy

As little data as possible, deleted after the blockade.

  • Under 15Consent from a parent or guardian is required at sign-up.
  • ModerationEvery message can be reported. Volunteers can remove a participant, and the team can ban an account.
  • Username onlyOther users never see a student's name or email address.
  • DeletionAll data is erased within 30 days after the blockade ends.

Personal data (GDPR)

Your data, explained simply.

This site is for high school students, some of them minors. We collect only what is necessary, we sell nothing, and we delete everything when the blockade ends.

  • What we collectA username, an email address, your track, your year and your specialty subjects. The messages you write in a lesson's group. Your IP address, in security logs.
  • What we do not ask forNo first or last name, no school, no date of birth.
  • What it is used forYour email confirms your account, sends you your login link again and warns you when a lesson changes. Your track, year and specialties show you the lessons that concern you. The IP address is used to fight abuse.
  • Who sees whatOther users only see your username. Your email is visible only to the moderation team, and every access is logged.
  • CookiesOne session cookie to keep you signed in, and two cookies to remember your language and theme. No advertising cookies, no audience measurement tools.
  • No ads, no trackingNo analytics tools, no advertising cookies.
  • How longUntil the blockade ends. Accounts, emails, messages, logs and backups are deleted within 30 days after that. Technical logs are kept for 30 days at most.
  • Your rightsYou can ask to access your data or to delete your account at any time. We reply within 30 days.

To exercise these rights or ask a question: soutienlyceen.blocus@gmail.com.
